About Me

Hi! I'm Zifei Li, a brand designer. In 2018 I returned to Shanghai after graduating from the Design Academy Eindhoven in the Netherlands and founded KAUKAU design studio, designing and upgrading the brand image for lululemon, nike, MAIA and other brands.

I focus on providing effective visual solutions, such as art direction, brand visuals, publications, images, photography, installations, and interface design,etc. I strive to explore innovative mediums to build connections with others via their unique visual thinking, taking advantage of cutting-edge technologies and different visual languages.
