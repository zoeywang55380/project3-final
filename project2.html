nike membership gwp

The NIKE Membership Growth Program is newly launched to motivate members to continue to participate and upgrade to have a stronger sense of belonging and identity. As a brand with true sports culture and genes, they want to reward members for their love of sports in the most NIKE sportsmanship way.

KAUKAU took inspiration from NKE's heritage according to the different levels of membership and used a series of classic statements as visual elements, placing them in different historical moments with the spirit of NKE, and using “glow” as a visual clue throughout the entire project, providing a complete set of four levels of membership gifts. A set of four levels of membership gifts were designed. The unique rewards were carefully crafted to convey the values that the brand cares about: "Every step of growth deserves to be rewarded.

In addition to being used in the design of member gifts, this visual presentation will also be extended in the promotional activities related to the online and offline stores of the Membership Growth Program.
