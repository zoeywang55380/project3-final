summer sweat games

In the heat of summer, lululemon invites the community to team up with their friends and partners to join the “2023 Summer Sweat Games” physical fitness challenge.

This physical fitness competition, which will be held since 2021, encourages people to challenge themselves and unleash their potential while sweating, and to connect with their partners in real life, accomplishing common growth with others.

In our opinion, the main visualization of “Summer Fun Challenge” should stay in the moment of “sweating in the summer heat”, and keep the dynamic moment of completing the challenge together with partners. Therefore, in KineticTypography's various attempts, we tried to find the best configuration of visual dynamics and semantics, using localized repetitions to express rhythm and convergence, which also means releasing the potential and inspiring people to participate in the challenge with visual fun.
